I still don't understand how this opinion is a minority opinion. It seems like wherever I go and discuss AI with people, there is very little conversation about how much you can do with local AI models. And there also is very little conversation about what that might mean for most companies.

## Are local LLM's any good?
I think people underestimate how much utility they can get just from downloading models via a service like Ollama. I run the 27B parameter Gemma3 on my laptop and in my experience it gives me roughly 80% of the utility that Gemini 3 gies me. And quite frankly, I think it surpasses answers from Chat GPT 5.

You know, you can talk about benchmarks all you want, but I have used Gemma for extensive research and coding projects (always fact checking it) and it is roughly on par with Gemini without any upgrades. Now, it might be that most LLM providers now route you to less powerful models in order to provide cheaper compute. So it is possible I'm just getting Gemma served from a Google server when using Gemini, but uhhh... What difference does it make then? If I get roughly the same quality using a model that runs on my Macbook Pro vs Google, what difference does it actually make?

## Scaling for nothing
If local LLM's are good enough, we are scaling for nothing.
